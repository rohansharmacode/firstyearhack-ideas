# Receipt spending insights

**Status:** Runner-up

## Old thing
Paper receipts and the shoebox (or the old personal budget book).

## 2026 twist
Snap a receipt, and the app tracks spending and answers questions like "how much did I spend on takeaway this month?"

## Snowflake use
Receipt line items in Snowflake. Cortex Analyst turns plain-English questions into SQL over the spending data.
