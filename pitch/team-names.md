# Team names and logo ideas

All original. Check the name is not a real game or brand before you commit.

| Name | Why it works | Logo idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kinetic Ring** | Says boxing and motion. Matches the game. | A neon boxing ring drawn as one glowing line, seen from above |
| **Snowpunch** | Snowflake plus punch. Judges remember it. | A snowflake whose six arms end in small gloves |
| **Shadowbox 2026** | Shadowboxing is real training, and you box your own shadow on camera | A glove outline with a pixel-art shadow |
| **No Controller** | The whole pitch in two words | A crossed-out retro controller in neon |
| **Jab Script** | Code joke (JavaScript), first-year friendly | `{ }` braces shaped like two gloves |
| **Frame Rate Fighters** | Retro arcade feel | 8-bit style text with a scanline effect |

**Recommendation:** "Snowpunch" as the team name (sponsor judges will smile), "Kinetic Ring" as the product name.

**Colour palette for the logo and deck:** deep navy `0B1026`, neon cyan `22E5FF`, hot pink `FF2E88`, white text.
